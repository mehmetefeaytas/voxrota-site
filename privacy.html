Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026

VoxRota is an offline-first audio application. We do not operate a server that receives your recordings, and the app does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Data the app uses

  • Audio recordings: created and stored only in the app's documents directory on your device. They leave the device only when you explicitly export or share them.
  • Microphone: used while you record. Permission is requested the first time you record.
  • Speech recognition: requested only when you create a transcript. On supported devices and languages, Apple's on-device recognition is used.
  • Purchases: VoxRota Pro is processed by Apple through StoreKit. We never receive your payment details.

Collection and sharing

We do not collect personal data, do not use third-party analytics, and do not share data with third parties. The app contains no advertising SDKs.

Storage and deletion

Projects remain on your device until you delete them in the app or remove the app. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to iOS backup behavior controlled by you.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new effective date.

Support

Questions, bug reports, and feature requests are welcome.

Contact

Open an issue on our public tracker: github.com/mehmetefeaytas/voxrota-site/issues.

Please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Do not attach recordings that contain sensitive information.

Purchases

VoxRota Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase handled by Apple. To restore it, open Settings → VoxRota Pro → Restore Purchases inside the app. Refunds are managed by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Microphone and speech

The app requests microphone access for recording and speech recognition for on-device transcription. Both are optional; you can change permissions in iOS Settings.
